RallyCam privacy

RallyCam is a personal iPhone and Apple Watch app that streams our table tennis games to YouTube and keeps the score.

Your Google account

When you sign in with YouTube, RallyCam asks for permission to manage your YouTube account. It uses that only to create, start and end live broadcasts on your own channel and to add chapters to their descriptions. The sign-in token is kept in the iPhone's Keychain and is sent only to Google. RallyCam has no server of its own, and nothing from your Google account is sent anywhere else.

You can sign out in the app's Settings at any time, and remove RallyCam's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Matches and clips

Scores, player names, match history and saved clips stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Video goes to YouTube only while you are streaming.

Shared match pages

A shared match link carries the match inside the link itself, after the #. Browsers do not send that part to this website, so the match is never stored here. If the match has a YouTube video, the page plays it with YouTube's embedded player, which is covered by Google's privacy policy.
