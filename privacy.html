RallyCam privacy

RallyCam is a personal iPhone and Apple Watch app that streams our table tennis games to YouTube and keeps the score.

Your Google account

When you sign in with YouTube, RallyCam uses that account to manage your broadcasts and fetch video start times. The sign-in token is kept in the iPhone's Keychain. When creating a short share link, the token is also sent over HTTPS to RallyCam's sharing service, which verifies your channel with Google before accepting the collection. The sharing service does not save the token.

You can sign out in the app's Settings at any time, and remove RallyCam's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Matches and clips

Your local match history and saved clips stay on your devices. Creating a share link uploads only the selected games' names, scores, point history, video IDs and playback timing to the sharing service. It does not upload clip files. Video goes to YouTube while you are streaming.

Shared match pages

New short links contain a random collection ID. The selected games are saved in RallyCam's sharing database and loaded when someone opens the link. Anyone with the link can view or forward the collection. There is no public list of games, and the site requests exclusion from search indexing. Shared collections are snapshots and remain available when local games are edited or deleted.

Older links carry the match data after # and still work without database storage. Match data is not committed to the public GitHub repository. If a game has a YouTube video, the page plays it with YouTube's embedded player, which is covered by Google's privacy policy.
